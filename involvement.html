Supporting Women in Computing (SWIC)

Vice President

Fall 2026 – Present

As Vice President of Supporting Women in Computing at Farmingdale State College, I help create opportunities for women interested in technology to connect, learn, and support one another. Being part of SWIC has allowed me to develop my leadership skills while helping build a welcoming community for students.

My Responsibilities

  • Help plan and organize club meetings, activities, and campus events.
  • Collaborate with club officers to coordinate events and encourage student participation.
  • Support initiatives that empower women pursuing careers in computing and technology.
  • Help create opportunities for members to network, share experiences, and build confidence.
  • Promote club events and help maintain an active and welcoming student community.

Treasurer

Spring 2026

As Treasurer of Supporting Women in Computing, I was responsible for managing the club's finances, tracking expenses, and organizing the budget for meetings and events. I helped make sure funds were used responsibly and that we stayed within our budget while planning activities for members.
