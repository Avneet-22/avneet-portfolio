About Me

Hi! I'm Avneet Kaur, a senior at Farmingdale State College majoring in Computer Programming and Information Systems. I enjoy learning about technology, developing new skills, and exploring different opportunities in the tech industry.

I'm also the Vice President of Supporting Women in Computing (SWIC). I believe it's important to support and encourage women pursuing careers in technology, especially in a field where women are often underrepresented. I'm grateful to be part of a community where women can empower one another, share experiences, build confidence, and grow together.

Being surrounded by women who share similar interests and goals has made my college experience even more meaningful. I'm excited to continue learning, making connections, and supporting other women in computing.
